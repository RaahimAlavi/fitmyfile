Privacy Policy for FitMyFile

Last updated: November 2026

Key Summary: FitMyFile is a 100% local, on-device utility. Your photos, documents, and compressed files never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server.

1. Information Collection and Processing

FitMyFile processes selected images and PDF documents entirely locally on your device. We do not operate external processing servers, and we do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your personal files, file names, or biometric data.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

FitMyFile displays advertisements using Google AdMob. To serve contextual or personalized ads, Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and app interaction telemetry in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Your photos and documents are never accessible to Google AdMob. All file operations remain strictly offline on your device.

To learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services, visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to retrieve advertisement content.
  • Storage / Storage Access Framework: The app accesses only the specific photos or PDF files you deliberately choose to pick and save via Android's secure system picker.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because FitMyFile does not collect or transmit personal file data, no user data is stored on remote servers. All temporary cached data generated during compression is discarded or remains within app-private local sandbox storage.

5. Children’s Privacy

FitMyFile does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact developer support at: support@fitmyfile.app
